# Keep the ledger in the open air

Made-up body for ADR-003.

## Context

The ledger of berths lives in a shed. Rain has spoiled two pages.

## Decision

The ledger would be moved to a stand in the open air.

## Rejected alternatives

A bigger shed costs too much. A copy kept in town goes out of date.

## Consequences

Clerks write in the wind. Pages need clips and a cover.

## Sources

A list of the pages lost to rain and a note from the clerk.

## Verification

Leave a test page on the stand for a month. Check that the ink stays clear.

## Invariants

The ledger has one copy at the quay. No page leaves the stand.
