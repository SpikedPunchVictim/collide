# Keep the quays flat

Made-up body for ADR-001.

## Context

The quays sit at one height. Boats of every size tie up to them.

## Decision

Every quay stays flat. None gets a step or a slope.

## Rejected alternatives

A stepped quay cost too much to build. A sloped quay let the ropes slip.

## Consequences

Small boats sit low against the wall. Crews carry cargo up by hand.

## Sources

The old tide table and the notes of the quay keeper.

## Verification

Walk each quay at low and at high tide. Check that a rope stays taut.

## Invariants

Every quay has one height. No quay is wider than ten metres.
