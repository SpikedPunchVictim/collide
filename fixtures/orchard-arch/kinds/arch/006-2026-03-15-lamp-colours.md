# Choose the lamp colours

Made-up body for ADR-006. Its evidence names a spike that is not in the tree.

## Context

The lamps along the quays all burn white. Boats cannot tell one quay from another.

## Decision

Each quay gets its own lamp colour. The colours are red, green and amber.

## Rejected alternatives

Numbered lamps are hard to read from far off. Lamps that blink need wiring that is too costly.

## Consequences

The lamp maker must stock three kinds of glass. Crews learn the colours in a day.

## Sources

A note from the lamp maker and a sketch of the harbor.

## Verification

Sail past each quay at night and name its colour. Ask a new crew to do the same.

## Invariants

No two quays share a colour. A lamp is never changed without a note in the log.
