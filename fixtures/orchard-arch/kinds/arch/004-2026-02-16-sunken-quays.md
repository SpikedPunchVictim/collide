# Sink the quays a little

Made-up body for ADR-004.

## Context

Flat quays flood at the highest tides. The water covers the cargo.

## Decision

The quays are sunk a little so the water runs off to the sides.

## Rejected alternatives

Raising the quays blocked the view of the water. Walls along the edge were too slow to build.

## Consequences

Cargo stays dry on most days. The edges need a drain to be cleaned each week.

## Sources

The tide readings of one winter and a price list for the digging.

## Verification

Pour water on a sunk quay and time how long it takes to clear. Repeat at each quay.

## Invariants

No quay holds standing water for more than an hour. Every drain has a cover.
