# Price berths by the metre

Made-up body for ADR-002.

## Context

Berths were priced by the boat. Owners of long boats paid the same as owners of short ones.

## Decision

A berth costs a fixed sum for each metre of the boat. The sum is set once a year.

## Rejected alternatives

A flat fee was unfair to small boats. A price by weight needed a scale at every berth.

## Consequences

Long boats pay more. The keeper measures each boat once.

## Sources

Three trial price lists and the tally of boats that stayed.

## Verification

Compare the year's takings with the old flat fee. Ask ten owners if the price is fair.

## Invariants

A price is always a whole number. Two boats of one length pay the same.
