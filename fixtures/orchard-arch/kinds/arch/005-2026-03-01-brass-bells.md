# Ring brass bells at dusk

Made-up body for ADR-005.

## Context

Boats come in at dusk and cannot see the quay. A sound is needed to guide them.

## Decision

A brass bell rings at dusk from each end of the harbor.

## Rejected alternatives

A horn was too loud for the town. A flag cannot be seen after dark.

## Consequences

Someone must ring the bell each evening. The brass needs polish.

## Sources

A letter from a boat owner and the list of boats that came in late.

## Verification

Stand at the far end of the harbor and listen for the bell. Note which evenings it is missed.

## Invariants

The bell rings at the same hour each day. The two bells never ring together.
